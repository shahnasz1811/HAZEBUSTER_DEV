1. HOW TO SET UP THE LEVEL-UP & HUD UI (1-CLICK METHOD)
================================================================================

We built an automatic UI builder directly inside your Unity Editor!

STEP-BY-STEP:
1. Open your HAZEBUSTER project in Unity.
2. In Unity's top menu bar, click:
   HazeBuster -> Create Complete Game UI & Level-Up Menu
3. That is literally it! 
   It will instantly build:
   - A Canvas (HUD_Canvas) scaled to 1920x1080.
   - EventSystem (for mouse clicking).
   - Top-Left: Health Bar (Red) + HP numbers.
   - Top-Center: EXP Bar (Blue) + Level Text ("Lv. 1") + Survival Clock ("00:00").
   - Bottom-Left: Dash charge indicator ("Dash: 1 / 1").
   - Top-Right: Dew counter ("Dew: 0").
   - The Level-Up Modal with 3 interactive Upgrade Cards!
   - Everything is already wired into the _UIManager component.

2. HOW THE LEVEL-UP PAUSE & 3-CARD UPGRADE SYSTEM WORKS
================================================================================

When you absorb enough Morning Dew drops to fill your blue EXP bar:
1. Level-Up Event Triggers:
   - Your level increases (Lv. 1 -> Lv. 2).
   - A cheerful 8-bit fanfare chime plays.
   - GameManager automatically sets the game state to "LevelingUp".
   - Time.timeScale is instantly set to 0.0 (THE GAME FULLY PAUSES!).
     * All burning enemies freeze in place.
     * All fireballs, embers, and projectiles pause mid-air.
     * The survival clock pauses.
     * Player movement is locked.

2. The 3 Upgrade Cards Appear:
   - The Level-Up Modal opens over the screen with a dark translucent haze backdrop.
   - UpgradeManager rolls 3 random valid perks from your pool, such as:
     * "Rapid Pressure Valve" (+25% Water Gun firing speed)
     * "Dual Stream Splitter" (+1 extra water stream in fan spread)
     * "Hydro-Vacuum Tank" (+1.2m Dew Pickup Range!)
     * "Halogen High-Beam" (+2.0m Vision Light circle through haze)
     * "Hydro-Dash Trail" (Dashing quenches burning peat on the ground)
     * "Deploy Peat Sprinkler" (Unlocks the 360-degree rotating nozzle)
     * "Chemical Foam Mortar" (Unlocks the extinguishing foam launcher)

3. Selection & Unpause:
   - Hover your mouse over any of the 3 cards (the card highlights).
   - Click your chosen card!
   - The selected upgrade is permanently added to your run.
   - The modal closes.
   - Time.timeScale is restored to 1.0 (GAME UNPAUSES SMOOTHLY!).

3. EXP TIERS & THE NEW PICKUP RANGE MECHANIC
================================================================================

In response to your feedback, EXP drops are NO LONGER automatically vacuumed
into the player from across the screen!

A. TIGHT BASE PICKUP RANGE:
   - The base pickup radius is now set to a tight 1.2 meters (you have to walk
     over to or touch the dew drop to collect it).
   - Defeated embers and stumps "pop" outward slightly onto the peat floor
     and rest there waiting for you.

B. IN-RUN PICKUP UPGRADES:
   - When leveling up, you can now choose the "Hydro-Vacuum Tank" upgrade!
   - Each time you take it, your pickup circle expands (+1.2m per rank).
   - By late game, you can upgrade it to pull in drops from meters away!

C. 3-TIER VISUAL DEW DROPS:
   - Tier 1: Small Dew Drop (Cyan / Light Blue) = 1 EXP
     (Dropped by common airborne embers)
   - Tier 2: Medium Dew Crystal (Emerald Green / Larger) = 5 EXP
     (Dropped by mid-game subterranean Gambut monsters)
   - Tier 3: Large Pure Moisture Core (Golden / Glowing) = 20 EXP
     (Dropped by heavy charred Burned Stumps and the Wildfire Boss!)

4. HOW DIFFICULTY SCALING OVER TIME WORKS IN HAZEBUSTER
================================================================================

Just like in "Everything is a Crab" and "Vampire Survivors", the survival
run operates on an escalating timeline:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TIMELINE PHASE        | ENEMY COMPOSITION           | DIFFICULTY BEHAVIOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Phase 1: Spark Spotting| 100% Airborne Embers        | Spawn every 1.8s.
(0:00 - 1:30)         | (Fast, low HP, 1 EXP drop)  | Easy to kite and douse.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Phase 2: Smoldering Stumps| 60% Embers, 40% Stumps   | Spawn interval drops to 1.2s.
(1:30 - 3:30)         | Heavy charred tree tanks    | Stumps emit smoke puffs that
                      | (High HP, drops 20 EXP drop)| block vision light.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Phase 3: Gambut Inferno| 30% Embers, 35% Stumps,     | Spawn interval drops to 0.7s.
(3:30 - 5:00)         | 35% Subterranean Gambut     | Ground becomes littered with
                      | (Leaves burning peat ground)| burning soil; requires dashing!
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Climax: Wildfire Nexus | MEGA FIRE BOSS SPAWNS!      | Spawn interval drops to 0.35s.
(5:00 mark)           | Multi-phase boss battle     | Constant ember storm + fire
                      | Fire breath & Fireball Novas| nova rings. Victory on kill!
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

HP SCALING:
Every 60 seconds that passes, all newly spawned enemies gain +12% maximum health,
simulating hotter, drier drought conditions in the peatland!

5. RECOMMENDED TUNING VALUES (SIZE, SPEED & COMBAT FEEL)
================================================================================

To achieve the punchy, satisfying feel of "Everything is a Crab":

A. PLAYER CHARACTER:
   - Move Speed: 5.5 to 6.0 units/sec (fast, snappy, responsive).
   - Dash Speed: 16 units/sec over 0.2s duration.
   - Dash Cooldown: 1.5s base (reduced by upgrades).
   - Sprite Scale: (1.0, 1.0, 1.0).

B. WATER GUN (PRIMARY WEAPON):
   - Projectile Speed: 15.0 units/sec (fast, straight water jet).
   - Base Fire Rate: 2.5 shots/sec (feels like continuous pressurized squirts).
   - Base Damage: 15 HP (extinguishes early embers in 1 shot).
   - Knockback Force: 6.0 (visibly pushes burning hazards backward).

C. ENEMIES:
   - Ember (Swarmer): Speed 3.6, HP 15 (fast, but slower than player's 5.5 speed).
   - Burned Stump (Tank): Speed 1.2, HP 80 (slow, intimidating, takes 5-6 shots).
   - Gambut (Creeper): Speed 2.0, HP 45 (leaves burning floor hazards).

D. MAIN CAMERA:
   - Projection: Orthographic
   - Size: 6.5 to 7.0 (gives a great panoramic view of the dark hazy forest).
   - Smooth Time in CameraFollow.cs: 0.10 (buttery smooth camera glide).
